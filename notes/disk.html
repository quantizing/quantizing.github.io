Mirrors of the disk

Hyperbolic geometry

Choose a point in an octagon. Eight rotations and eight reflections generate its orbit. A point on a mirror has fewer distinct images.

Orbit size in the central octagon · 16

Where AdS enters

A constant-time slice of global AdS3 has the geometry of this Poincaré disk. Its ideal rim is the spatial circle of the boundary CFT. Inside, a regular {8,3} tiling puts three octagons at every vertex. Reflection across an edge is a hyperbolic isometry.
