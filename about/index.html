About me

I work in condensed matter theory. My main interests are conformal field theory, quantum foundations, and topology.

The diagram on my homepage follows the same patch of spins through three length scales. It’s a small example of a question I like: how much can we forget about the microscopic details and still understand the physics?

I’m also drawn to high-energy physics, especially gravity and black holes. I’d like to understand whether ideas from quantum matter might one day help me think about those problems.
