Reading the lattice at three scales

For the diagram on my homepage, I used one configuration of the square-lattice Ising model. Each site carries a spin of +1 or −1. Silver marks +1 and graphite marks −1. The outlined block marks the same patch of the system on all three layers.

One rule, applied twice

The fine layer has 81 × 81 spins. Replace each 3 × 3 block by its majority spin to obtain the 27 × 27 layer. Repeating the rule gives the 9 × 9 layer. A selected patch contains 81 microscopic spins, nine intermediate spins, and one final spin. The live readout counts the nine intermediate votes and shows their majority.

Where CFT enters

At criticality, the long-distance limit of the two-dimensional Ising model is described by a conformal field theory. Blocking gives a way to look for structure across scales. A single finite configuration illustrates the operation; establishing conformal invariance concerns the scaling limit of observables.

Model and sampling details

Nearest-neighbour ferromagnetic coupling, zero external field and periodic boundaries. The dimensionless coupling is set to the infinite-lattice critical value, K = J/(kBT) = ½ log(1 + √2). A Wolff cluster update generates each successive configuration. Starting from random spins with seed 240319, the figure uses update 1514, selected after 1500 warm-up updates for visibility of both signs on the coarsest layer.
